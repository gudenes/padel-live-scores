Bottom Nav Animations

Four takes on playful & fast. Tap any tab in each demo to feel it. Goal: instant feedback, a hint of disorder, never sluggish.

Option A — Pop & Settle
SPRING

Bar pops in with a 15% overshoot. Icon kicks up, rotates, and spring-settles. Label letters cascade down 1 by 1. Confident & bouncy without feeling chaotic.

Option B — Scatter & Settle
DISORGANIZED

Bar slams down from above squashing. Icon wobbles like it got tackled. Five tiny confetti particles burst out at staggered times. Letters arrive jittered & rotated. Most playful — borderline rowdy.

PAGE CONTENT
Option C — Ink Slide
PHYSICAL

A single green bar physically slides from the old tab to the new one, squashing on impact. New icon kicks up; old icon falls down. Feels like a pinball — directional, spatially tied to where you came from.

PAGE CONTENT
Option D — Ink Slide + Spring
A × C

The ink bar slides between tabs (C's directional clarity) with a stretchy overshoot landing. Layered on top: the new icon does A's spring kick + rotate and the label cascades letter-by-letter. Old icon takes a soft fall. Spatial story + personality.
