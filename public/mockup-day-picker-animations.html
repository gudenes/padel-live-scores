Day Picker Animations

Three takes on porting Option D (ink-slide + spring) to the matches day-strip. Tap any pill or arrow in each demo. Same sub-500ms budget; same instant color flip on tap.

Option α — Sliding Chunky Shadow
CLOSEST TO TODAY

The lime offset-shadow that already sits behind the active pill becomes a single shared element that physically slides from the old pill to the new one. Spring kick on the new pill; soft fall on the old. Letter cascade on the weekday + day number. Evolves the existing aesthetic — minimal disruption.

Option β — Sliding Underline Bar
TAB-STYLE

A thin lime bar tracks the active pill from below — exactly the nav-D bar idiom. Pill backgrounds stay flat; the bar IS the active indicator. Lighter visual weight, reads as a tab indicator. Pill still does a small spring kick so the tap feels live.

Option γ — Lift & Fall
MOST PLAYFUL

No sliding marker — the pill itself is the marker. Active pill lifts and tilts (-3°, translateY -7px, scale 1.13); previous pill falls back. Lime shadow fades in locked to the new active. Fewest moving parts but the most "physical kick" on tap.

All three play under 0.5s. The active state's color flip is synchronous with the tap — motion plays on top. Honors prefers-reduced-motion.
