My take

Option C — Stacked offset is the most on-brand. It leans into the chunky aesthetic instead of softening it, gives the active pill genuine prominence (more than the current solid fill, IMO), and the layered look reads as a "raised tile" — fits a sport-scoreboard energy.

Option B — Frame & Glow is the safest premium-feel option. Calmer than the current solid fill, lets the rest of the page breathe.

Option D — Tooth tab is the most subtle. Best if you want the day pills to feel like a secondary nav rather than a primary control. Probably too quiet for the matches surface.
