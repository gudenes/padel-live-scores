Design Notes
Key decisions in the light palette:
• Base is warm off-white (#F7F6F3), not pure white — easier on the eyes, feels premium
• Cards are true white (#FFFFFF) with subtle shadow (0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)) for separation
• Brand green darkened from #7ED321 to #4A8C12 — passes WCAG AA on white
• Orange darkened from #F5A623 to #D4870F — same reason
• Live red shifted from #FF4655 to #DC2626 — more serious, better contrast
• Men's blue: #4A9EFF to #2563EB / Women's purple: #D966FF to #7C3AED
• Pair colors: Orange #D4580A / Yellow #B8860B (darker for legibility)
• Clip-paths preserved identically — the chunky brand identity works in both modes
• W badge, LIVE pill, CTA buttons keep white text on colored bg in both modes
• Loser names use lighter gray (#9CA3AF) in light mode for natural dimming
