What changed

One list, by time

Court sub-sections (Center → Court 2 → Court 3) replaced with a single chronological flow. Easier to answer "what's next?" without reading three court columns.

Live in its time slot

Live matches stay in their natural slot. The red LIVE chip on the card already provides the visual anchor — no "live" section header needed.

Finished at the bottom

A subtle divider separates finished matches. They're slightly faded but fully visible. Most-recent finish first.

The MatchCard itself is unchanged — court chip, time, scores all in their existing positions. Only the parent layout changes.

Tournament header

Unchanged. Same flag, name, status pill, count, chevron. Sort order of tournaments on the day page is unaffected.
