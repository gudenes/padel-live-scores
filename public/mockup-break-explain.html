Break — clarity options

Today: a small red "B" badge sits above the bar of the team that won a game while their opponent was serving. Users have to learn what "B" means. Four ways to make it self-evident, all on the same Brussels P2 SF data.
Option A · Word "BROKE" instead of letter
The badge becomes a tiny pill that literally reads BROKE — no glossary needed. Slightly wider but unambiguous: a glance tells you what happened.
