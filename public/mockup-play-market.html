The loop

One binary question at a time, priced by the crowd and anchored to our own Elo model. Swipe to take a side, choose a stake, and the position stays tradeable until the market resolves. Every card below is a market our database can settle on its own — no operator judgement required.

1
Discover
A new market every swipe. The horizon chip — LIVE, MATCH, TOURNAMENT, SEASON — tells you how long your money is committed before it can resolve.
2
Swipe right for Yes
Confidence expressed as a gesture. The card tilts, the YES stamp bleeds in, and the border picks up the lime as you cross the commit threshold.
3
Swipe left for No
Same physics, red stamp. Taking the No side is a first-class action, not a rejection — it's how the underdog price gets made.
4
Choose amount
Price per share is the crowd probability expressed in Guacas. 250 Guacas at 0.38 buys 658 shares, each worth 1.00 if the market resolves your way.
5
You're in
Confirmation plus an immediate next card. The deck never dead-ends — that's what keeps session length up.
6
Track & trade
Open positions revalue as prices move. Sell early to lock a gain, or ride it to resolution.
7
Activity
Other people's trades, arriving live. ADI shows anonymous wallet hashes because on-chain it has no choice — we have accounts, so the feed carries names and becomes social instead of just noisy. Big stakes get flagged, so you can see where conviction is.
8
Leaders
Ranked by net worth — balance plus the live value of open positions — so holding a winning position counts before it resolves. Your own row stays pinned at the bottom no matter how far down you are.

Three deliberate changes from the reference

No currency symbols. The reference showed P&L as €0.54. In a no-money product a € or $ anywhere is a regulatory and trust problem — P&L here is shown in G only.

Yes and No always sum to 100. ADI PredictStreet shows 54% / 47% because it bakes a spread into each side. A play-money market has no house cut, so anything but 100 just reads as broken arithmetic.

The model is visible, not hidden. Every card carries our Elo number next to the crowd price. The gap between them is the whole story — and it's a signal no competitor can copy.
