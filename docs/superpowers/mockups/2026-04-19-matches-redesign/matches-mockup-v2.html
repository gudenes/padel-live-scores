What's new in v2

  • Today centered: the date strip is centre-justified with two days before + two days after. Today sits in the middle with a green underline (the previous version left-aligned "Today" which pushed it toward the edge).
  • Tournament logos: each card has a chunky-polygon logo tile on the left (colored disk with initials in the mockup — in production, pulls from tournament.logo_url with a text-fallback). A small country flag sits next to the tournament name as secondary info.
  • Circuit sections: ● PREMIER PADEL is always the first section; ● FIP TOUR follows. Each has a count ("3 events") and a fade rule to the right for visual separation.
  • Circuit dot on logo: each tournament logo has a small diamond marker in its top-right corner — green for Premier, orange for FIP — so circuit identity survives even if you ever scroll past the section header.

Sort order (locked)

  1. Premier Padel section — always on top. Within it: live first, then upcoming by kickoff, then finished.
  2. FIP Tour section — same internal ordering.
  3. Further circuits (future: APT, WPT) append after FIP.
