Real match: Brussels P2 SF — Coello/Tapia 7-5 6-2 Yanguas/Stupaczuk. Bars are real per-game points. Breaks marked are realistic (server losing serve). Four visual treatments below — pick the one that fits.
Option A · Compact "B" badge
Small chunky badge with the letter "B" sitting above whichever bar broke serve. Cheap on real-estate, scannable, doesn't clutter the chart.
