Lime green variations

G1 — Lime offset shadow (current)
CHUNKY

Active pill drops to the page-bg and shows a chunky lime offset shadow. Distinctive but feels different from the rest of the app.

G2 — Solid lime fill
CTA-STYLE

Active pill is fully lime, dark text — the same treatment as primary CTAs across the app (login, follow, etc). Most consistent with site language.

G3 — Lime tint
SOFT

Semi-transparent lime wash + lime border + lime top label. Lighter weight than full fill, still clearly active.

G4 — Lime top stripe
TAB-INDICATOR

Pill stays neutral; a thin lime bar above the pill carries the active state — same idiom as the bottom-nav active indicator.

G5 — Lime ring glow
FOCUS-RING

Lime border + soft outer glow (3px box-shadow at 18% opacity). Reads as "focused" rather than "filled".

Alternative palettes
