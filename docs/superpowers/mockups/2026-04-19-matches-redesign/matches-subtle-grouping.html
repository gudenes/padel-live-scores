How they compare

A · Light header

Quickest to scan for "which tournament am I in." Still looks like a header — just no dark block behind it. Closest to iOS/SofaScore section labels. Recommended default.

B · Left gutter

Most distinctive — matches ride beside a vertical gutter. Great when a tournament has many matches. Feels bespoke to padel. Risk: gutter content can cramp on small screens, and the short name has to wrap or truncate.

C · Inline label

Subtlest possible. Great if the Yesterday/Today/Upcoming filter already narrows the list short. Breaks down when a single tournament dominates (label repeats 20×).
