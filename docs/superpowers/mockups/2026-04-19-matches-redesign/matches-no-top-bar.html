Bars gone. Live state survives through three other signals: the top "Live Now · 2" strip, an inline "Live" badge next to the tournament name, and the per-match Live status pill inside the row. Green bar on non-live tournaments was always redundant — the tournament is already identified by its header.

My take as the reviewer

Yes, remove it. The green bar on non-live tournaments carries no information — it's just decoration that uses the brand colour in a place where it doesn't mean anything. The red bar on live tournaments is redundant with: the "Live Now · N" strip above, the row-level Live pill, and the inline Live badge we're adding here. Three signals for one state is overkill; two is healthy.

The bar was also the only thing using ::before with a hardcoded 2px height on the header — dropping it simplifies the component too.

What carries live state after the bar goes

  • Live Now · N strip at the top of Today (page-level signal)
  • Inline "Live" badge next to the tournament name on its header (group-level signal)
  • Per-match "Live · Set N" pill on each in-play row (match-level signal)
  • Current-set score in green on the scores column (still there)
  • Pulsing point score in red (still there)
