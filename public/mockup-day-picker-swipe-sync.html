Day Picker — swipe-sync animation

Drag the dark content area left/right (touch or mouse) to simulate swiping between days. Watch how the day picker reflects swipe progress in real time. Each row uses a different visualization. Or use the slider underneath to scrub progress at exact 0–100%.
