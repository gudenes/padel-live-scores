About this first version

Scores and video bookmarks stay in this Chrome profile and survive closing the panel. Pause/resume follows video time; pause does not end a rally. You may record an outcome while paused. Scouting records and calculated scores save to the server when admin is connected. The panel confirms each server save.

Live replay depends on the broadcaster’s available buffer. Reloading or replacing the video requires reconnecting; old bookmarks stay in your export, but replay in a new playback session is not yet supported.

Ads and broadcaster timeline changes may affect timing. Live timestamps are not guaranteed to match the later replay.
