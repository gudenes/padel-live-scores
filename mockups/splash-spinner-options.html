Splash spinner options

Logo shifted slightly above center (~30% from top). Spinner positioned in lower third — between logo bottom and screen edge. Each option uses the Padel Nachos lime (#7ED321) to stay on-brand.

A — Bouncing dots
Three lime dots bouncing in rhythm. Friendly, energetic. Used by WhatsApp Web's typing indicator.
RECOMMENDED
B — Spinning arc (Material)
Modern circular arc spinning fast (0.9s rotation). Clean, neutral, widely understood. Used by Stripe, Linear, GitHub.
C — Pulsing ring (orbital)
Lime core with expanding rings rippling outward. Calmer, more brand-distinctive. Used by Discord's voice-channel indicator.
D — Progress bar
Indeterminate progress bar sliding across. Linear feel, suggests forward motion. Used by Material Web.
E — Equalizer bars
Five lime bars bouncing like an audio EQ. Athletic, energetic. Distinctive but might feel music-app-coded.
F — Dual ring (counter-spinning)
Outer ring spins one direction, inner ring counter-spins. Premium feel. Used by Apple Music, Notion.
G — Native iOS (current)
12 lines, "rotating", flat. This is what you're seeing now. Looks dated.

All variants are pure CSS — no images, no Lottie, no JS. Implementation: splash starts as native (Capacitor SplashScreen), then once the WebView paints first frame, a full-screen HTML overlay with the chosen spinner takes over until the app is ready. Once we pick, expect ~30 min of work plus a new TestFlight build cycle.
