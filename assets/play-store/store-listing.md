## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Follow every padel point live.

Padel Nachos is the fastest way to track Premier Padel and FIP — real-time
scores, point-by-point updates, player rankings, tournament draws,
highlights, and breaking padel news, all in one app.

LIVE SCORES, POINT BY POINT
Watch matches unfold ball by ball. Live scores update the moment a point
is played, with set-by-set history, momentum charts, and full match stats
once the dust settles.

FOLLOW YOUR PLAYERS
Follow Coello, Tapia, Galán, Chingotto, Triay, Brea, and the rest of the
top 100 — men and women. Get push notifications when your players step
onto court and when they finish a match.

EVERY TOURNAMENT, EVERY ROUND
Premier Padel P1 and P2 events, FIP Gold/Silver/Bronze, Cupra FIP Tour,
Premier Padel Tour finals — full draws, schedules, results, prize money,
and Order of Play. See defending champions, draw size, and category
breakdowns at a glance.

OFFICIAL + RACE RANKINGS
Live FIP official and race rankings, men and women, top 1000. Track movers
of the week, see breakdowns by country, and tap any name to jump to a full
player profile with head-to-head, season stats, and equipment history.

NEWS YOU WANT TO READ
A personalized padel feed: match recaps, highlight videos, interviews,
and tournament reports. The feed learns what you care about — your
favorite players, your language, men's or women's circuit — and surfaces
the stories that matter to you.

MATCH HIGHLIGHTS
Watch the best points and moments straight from the channels you trust:
official tournament uploads, top padel YouTubers, and curated highlights
from every Premier Padel event.

PREDICTIONS & STATS
Predict winners before each match. Track your prediction streak. Compare
players head-to-head. See season form, monthly performance, surface
breakdowns, and full career titles for every top player.

WHY PADEL NACHOS
- Real-time scores. No 30-second refresh delay — points appear as they
  happen.
- Beautiful, focused design built mobile-first.
- Built by padel fans, for padel fans.
- Free. No ads. No paywalls.

Padel Nachos is independent and not affiliated with the FIP, Premier
Padel, or any tournament organizer. All trademarks belong to their
respective owners.

Questions? hello@padelnachos.com
```
