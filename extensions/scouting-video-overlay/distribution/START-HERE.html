Padel Nachos Scout

Quick start · external tester · v{{VERSION}}

You need desktop Google Chrome and this ZIP package. No account or video is needed for practice. Real scouting requires an account with Scouter access.

1. Install

Setup helper: after extracting the ZIP into a permanent folder, double-click Install-on-Mac.command on Mac or Install-on-Windows.cmd on Windows. It opens Chrome’s extension manager and the package folder. You still need to enable Developer mode and select Load unpacked yourself. If your computer blocks the helper, use the manual steps below.

  1. Unzip padel-nachos-scout-{{VERSION}}.zip and keep its extracted folder somewhere permanent.
  2. Open Chrome and type chrome://extensions in the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode. Click Load unpacked and choose the padel-nachos-scout folder inside the extracted package—the folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Open Chrome’s extensions menu and pin Padel Nachos Scout. Click its icon to open the scouting panel. Check v{{VERSION}} at the bottom.

These steps follow Chrome’s installation instructions. If Developer mode is blocked on a work computer, ask its administrator.

2. Try the playground first

  1. Open the panel’s menu and select Playground. A separate practice tab opens with sample players and a simulated video clock.
  2. Confirm the players are on court, then select Start rally. Try Winner, Unforced and Forced; choose a stroke and press Space to save.
  3. Try smash attempts, assists, recovery, VAR, faults, changing server, switching ends and Undo. Switch between Pro and Expert between rallies. The keys appear on the controls; you can edit shortcuts in the menu.
  4. Select Reset to start again. Close the practice tab to return to the real scouting panel.

Practice is separate. Its scores, shortcuts and confirmations stay in memory in that practice tab. They are never uploaded. Reloading or closing the tab discards practice.

3. Scout a real match

  1. Ask Gustavo to grant Scouter access to your account in admin Team Access. Then sign in through the extension using that account.
  2. Select New match, find the match and follow its setup steps. You can also create a private scouting match.
  3. Open the match video in Chrome. Click the extension icon on that video tab, then Connect video.
  4. Confirm who is on court and who serves. Start a rally at the first serve, record its outcome and save the point. Repeat.
  5. After the match, confirm the score against the broadcast, use Sync now, and wait for Saved to server. Export a local backup and open the match report in admin.

If something does not work

  • Panel will not open: select a normal video webpage, then click the extension icon.
  • Video will not connect: click the extension icon on the video tab, then reconnect. Start with a YouTube replay for your first real session.
  • Controls are unavailable: check the court/server confirmations and start the rally.
  • Cannot save to server: confirm your Scouter access, sign in again and retry Sync now. Keep the local copy until the server confirms.

Updating: export a local backup first. Replace the files in the same extension folder, click Reload in chrome://extensions, refresh the video and reconnect. Keep Developer mode enabled. Do not uninstall or clear extension storage to update.
